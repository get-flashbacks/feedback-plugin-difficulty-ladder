Auto-adjust difficulty

Raise or lower the master-difficulty slider automatically between sections, based on your recent hit accuracy. Requires a note-detection scorer (e.g. the note_detect plugin) to be active, and only affects songs with a phrase-level difficulty ladder.

Glass-filling section HUD

Show upcoming song sections as glasses in the player — glass size reflects each section's peak difficulty, fill level reflects the current master-difficulty setting.

Sensitivity

2

How confident auto-adjust must be before it moves the slider, and how big a step it takes: cautious waits for a longer run of near-perfect (or rough) sections; aggressive reacts on less evidence and moves difficulty in bigger steps.

Reaction speed

2

How much weight a single section's result carries in your rolling accuracy average. Slow smooths over a longer run of sections before it counts; fast lets one great (or rough) section move the average sooner. Independent of Sensitivity above — this only changes how quickly evidence builds, not how confident auto-adjust needs to be. Default (2) matches this plugin's original, non-configurable behavior.

Difficulty bounds

Auto-adjust never pushes the difficulty outside this range, even on a long streak.

Generate ladder depth cap

4

Maximum difficulty tiers "⚙️ Generate Difficulties" can give a phrase when building a ladder for a song that doesn't have one yet — simple phrases still get a short ladder, complex ones can use up to this many tiers.

Auto-adjust pauses itself whenever you move the difficulty slider manually — click the player's "Auto-Difficulty" pill to resume it.
